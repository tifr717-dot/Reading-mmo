Reading MMO v5.9.7 — Active Adventure HUD

- Adds a compact Active Adventure HUD inside the Read tab's Reading Session panel.
- Shows every active Hunt, Gather, and Alchemy quest while you read.
- Live page, timer, and chapter progress is projected before the session is banked.
- Prevents double-counting pages already committed with + Add to MMO.
- Distinguishes In Progress, Tracking Live, Ready When Session Ends, Verify Requirement, and Ready to Turn In.
- Session/round-based quests note that the current round is banked when End Session is pressed.
- Quest Board shortcut opens directly from the HUD.
- HUD refreshes every second and immediately after quest roll/clear/turn-in.
- No changes to the established Home, Quests, Play, or Me compositions.
