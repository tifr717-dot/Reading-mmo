Reading MMO v5.9.6 — Role Turn-In Integration

- Requirements Met buttons are now role-specific: Turn In & Fight / Turn In & Gather / Turn In & Brew.
- Turn-in calls the existing Hunt, Gather, or Alchemy resolution directly.
- The quest's selected mob, area, or recipe is restored before resolution.
- Direct Play completion buttons respect tracked reading requirements instead of bypassing them.
- Successful tracked turn-ins add a compact Adventure Recap to the existing result sequence.
- Subjective flavor requirements still require verification before turn-in.
- Visual layouts are unchanged from v5.9.5.
